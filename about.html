01 / Hello

Hi, I am Rafia. This is how I got here and what I am working on.

I am a Computer Science student at NED University in Karachi, Pakistan, with a specialization in Gaming and Animation.

Most of my hands on work lives in Unity and Unreal Engine: 2D, 3D and immersive VR projects, plus foundational software and games written in Python and C++.

I care about the parts that make a game feel right. Core gameplay mechanics, character animation, physics systems and the small problems nobody sees until they break.

I am open to game development internships, gameplay programming projects and technical collaborations.
